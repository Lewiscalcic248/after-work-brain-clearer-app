## ✨ Core Features

The After Work Brain Clearer helps you manage your day without the noise of modern digital tools.

### Natural Language Task Input
Type your tasks as you think them. The system understands time-based phrases. You can type "Review project report tomorrow at 9 AM" and the system creates a scheduled entry for you. It handles Chinese language inputs to assist with accurate time parsing. 

### Local-First Storage
Your information stays on your computer. You own your data. The application does not send your tasks to a cloud server. This provides privacy and ensures you can access your list even if your internet connection stops working.

### Offline Reliability
This tool runs inside your browser but behaves like a native desktop app. You can plan your day at home or during your commute. You do not need a login or an account to use the features. 

### Focus-Driven Design
The interface removes distractions. You see your daily schedule and nothing else. This helps you finish your tasks and leave work at work. The design keeps your workflow simple and direct.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues, check these steps first:

- The app does not open: Ensure you installed the latest version from the link above. Restart your computer if the installer fails to respond.
- The browser shows a blank page: Refresh the page by pressing F5 on your keyboard. Ensure your browser is up to date.
- Data does not save: Check that your hard drive has sufficient storage space. The application stores data in your user profile folder.
- Installation warnings: Windows might show a "SmartScreen" alert because the app is new. Click "More info" and then click "Run anyway" to finish the installation.

## 🔐 Privacy and Security

Your privacy drives the design of this software. Since the application uses local storage, no third party sees your thoughts or your task list. Your computer holds all records in a private database file. You can back up this file by copying it to an external drive or a secure location on your machine.
